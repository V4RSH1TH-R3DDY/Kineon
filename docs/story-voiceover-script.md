# Raghu's Day: voiceover script (4:00)

One narrator, about 528 words. Each block matches a scene in the video; start each block when its scene begins. Read calmly at about 140 words a minute.

## 0:00–0:06 · Title card (no voiceover)

*Silent. Title card: Team Rutherfordium, Kineon, and the team members.*

## 0:06–0:26 · Brushing his teeth

This is Raghu. He's sixty-four, and he has lived with Parkinson's for six years. His day starts like anyone's, brushing his teeth. His wrist is moving fast, and a basic tracker would call that a bad tremor. Kineon's model recognises it as everyday movement, so it isn't counted.

## 0:26–0:48 · Loose strap at breakfast

At breakfast, his watch strap has worked loose, and the watch is sliding around his wrist. Kineon can't trust that signal, so it doesn't take a reading. Instead, it sends a message to his phone: tighten the strap. It never fills the gap with a zero. He tightens it, and measuring starts again.

## 0:48–1:02 · Logging the morning dose

At eight, he takes his tablets and logs the dose with one tap. He adds a tag for his morning coffee. Every dose and every tag sits beside his readings.

## 1:02–1:14 · The walk

Then he heads out for his walk around the lake. Walking isn't tremor either, so it's set aside. But Kineon keeps measuring quietly, fifty times a second, all day.

## 1:14–1:32 · The tremor

Halfway round, his right hand starts to shake, harder than usual. His watch buzzes. Even while he's walking, Kineon picks the tremor out of the movement: a score of fifty-eight, against his usual thirty-one. That's high, and it's the third time this week.

## 1:32–1:54 · Priya sees it live

At the same moment, across the city, his daughter Priya gets an alert. She's his emergency contact. She opens the app and sees her father's readings live, straight from his watch: what's happening now, and how his day has gone. She doesn't have to guess. She calls him.

## 1:54–2:18 · Calm Mode and his stats

Raghu sits down on a bench. His watch offers Calm Mode, and he follows it: arm resting, breathing in on one tap, out on two. Then he opens his stats. The pattern is clear. His tremor is higher and more frequent than usual, and it keeps starting about three hours after a dose.

## 2:18–2:38 · Booking his neurologist

Parkinson's has no cure, so Raghu has seen the same neurologist for six years. Dr. Meera Rao is saved in the app as his doctor. He picks an earlier appointment, attaches his last thirty days, and books. Priya gets the confirmation too.

## 2:38–2:56 · Arriving at the clinic

On Thursday, Dr. Rao welcomes him in. Before she sees anything, the app asks Raghu for his permission to share his data with her clinic. He can take that permission back at any time, with one tap. He agrees.

## 2:56–3:18 · The examination

Dr. Rao examines him as she always does, and asks him to hold out his arm. Then she opens Kineon and downloads his chart. It's signed, so she knows nothing in it has been changed. Instead of one fifteen-minute snapshot, she has a whole month.

## 3:18–3:42 · The doctor decides

The chart shows what Raghu couldn't describe. His tremor drops after each dose, then climbs back before the next one is due. Dr. Rao recognises the wearing-off pattern, and she adjusts the timing of his doses. Kineon didn't diagnose anything. It gave her the evidence, and she made the decision. His new reminders arrive on his phone.

## 3:42–4:00 · Two weeks later

Two weeks later, the same walk around the lake. His readings are back within his usual range. One episode this week instead of three, and Priya and Dr. Rao can both see it. Kineon. Better data, so the doctor can decide.
